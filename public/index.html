Questions

Answers before you begin.

Do you handle both supply and installation?

Yes. ELV.art covers the full path — site survey, system design and BOQ, product supply, installation, testing, documentation, training, and ongoing support.

Which cities in Pakistan do you cover?

We deliver projects in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Sialkot, and surrounding regions.

Can you integrate CCTV, access control, and barriers together?

Absolutely. Our specialty is joining CCTV, access control, ANPR, barriers, networking, and automation into one monitored command view.

Do you offer maintenance after handover?

Yes — annual maintenance contracts (AMC), upgrades, and remote assistance keep your system reliable over its full life.
